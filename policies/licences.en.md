# Licences

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

## This repository

The text of this repository is shared under CC BY 4.0.
The full text is in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
The code of this repository is shared under the MIT License.
The full text is in [LICENSE-CODE](../LICENSE-CODE).
The forms that are identical to those in the operator's other repositories are under the same licences.

## Results of a collaboration

Text and papers that result from a collaboration are published under CC BY 4.0.
Code is published under the MIT License.
The operator does not send the results of a collaboration to a venue where they cannot be published under CC BY 4.0.

## Contributions

Text contributed by a collaborator is treated as contributed under CC BY 4.0.
Code contributed is treated as contributed under the MIT License.
The proposal form has a box that confirms this.
In the same box, the contributor confirms holding the right to release the contribution under that licence.
When a contribution contains another person's text or code, the form asks for its source and licence.

## Licences cannot be revoked

The CC BY 4.0 [LICENSE](../LICENSE) describes the grant as 「a worldwide, royalty-free, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, irrevocable license」.
The same document also states 「Our licenses are irrevocable.」
What has already been published stays under its licence after a collaboration stops.
Whether a grant can be undone when the contributor is under 18 years of age is not concluded on this page.
A proposal from such a person is accepted only with the consent of a legal representative (such as a person with parental authority).
The article concerned (Article 5 of the Civil Code) and the point left open are under "Civil Code" in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md).

## No DCO

This repository does not use the DCO (Developer Certificate of Origin) rule of adding a Signed-off-by line to commits.
That line leaves a name and an email address in the public history.
This repository asks for neither a real name nor contact details.
Instead, the confirmation box of the proposal form confirms the right to release the contribution under its licence.
Claude decided this on 2026-11-18.
