# Personal data

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

## Who handles it

This repository is run by the individual who holds the GitHub account cpsbvbng26-dotcom, Takuya Nemoto (根本卓哉).
The operator's address is not published.
It is given without delay to anyone who asks for it.
How to ask is under "Requests that cannot be made in public" in [Requests](./requests.en.md).
The reading of the law is under "Act on the Protection of Personal Information" in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md).

## What is collected

This repository handles what is written in its GitHub issue forms, and requests and reports received at the email address of the operator's [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md#information).
In an issue, it handles the GitHub account name and what is written in a form.
By email, it handles the sender's email address and what the email says.
It does not ask for a real name, an address, an affiliation or contact details.
It does not ask for special care-required personal information, such as medical history or the state of a person's body or mind.
The forms ask that these not be written.
This repository has no website, no cookie and no analytics.
GitHub handles the records of visits to its pages under its own statement.

## Purposes of use

- To read and answer proposals for collaboration.
- To carry out an agreed collaboration and record who did what.
- To answer requests for removal, correction, disclosure and the like.
- To handle error reports and notices from rights holders.

The operator uses the data for no other purpose.
What arrives at the email address is used for the same purposes only.
The section "Information" of the operator's Disclaimer also applies to issues opened with the error report form and to requests received by email.
The forms made for this repository state these purposes before their first field.

## What is public

Issues are public.
The person who writes an issue puts it on GitHub's public site.
GitHub (United States) stores and shows issues under its terms and statement.
The GitHub Terms of Service allow GitHub and its affiliates to use content, issues included, to train AI features and models.
The phrase is quoted under "GitHub Terms of Service" in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md).
The operator cannot stop this use.
The operator does not treat GitHub as a contractor handling personal data on the operator's behalf.

## Giving issues to a language model

The operator works on this repository with Claude Code.
Only for an issue whose consent box is ticked, the operator may give its title and text to Claude (Anthropic, United States).
The operator treats this as a provision to a third party in a foreign country under Article 28 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
The English sentence of the consent box is "I consent to the operator giving the title and text of this issue to Claude (Anthropic, United States)."
The system of the United States and Anthropic's measures are in the section "Issues and the language model" of the operator's [disclaimer](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md).
That section quotes the page of the Personal Information Protection Commission and Anthropic's privacy policy.
The proposal, request and rights-holder forms were made for this repository.
In these forms, the box is optional.
An issue without the tick is handled by the operator without giving it to Claude or any other language model.
The error report form is a word-for-word copy of the form in the operator's other repositories.
In that form, the consent box is required.
That form says that, without consent, the error can be reported by email instead, to the address the Disclaimer gives.
Comments on issues are not given to a language model.
The content of an email is not given to a language model either.
The consent can be withdrawn at any time, in an issue or by email.
After a withdrawal, the issue is not given to a language model.
Issues opened by the operator are outside this rule.

## Other third parties

The operator gives personal data to no other third party.
A provision based on laws and regulations is the exception.
The operator receives no personal data from third parties.

## How long it is kept

Issues are kept until a request for removal.
The names and roles of contributors are kept as the record of what was published.
On request, the way a name is shown is changed, or the name is removed.
A name printed in a draft already published cannot be changed by the operator.
The history of the repository, and copies already made by others, may still hold an entry.
GitHub notifies accounts that are set to receive notices of activity on an issue.
Depending on the receiver's settings, a notice arrives as an email that contains a copy of the issue.
Removing an issue does not necessarily remove copies already delivered as notices.

## Security measures

The operator does not gather what is written in issues anywhere else.
Apart from giving the title and text of an issue with consent to Claude, the operator makes no copies of it.
Copies delivered by GitHub's notices are as set out in "How long it is kept" above.
Only the operator's GitHub account can change this repository.
Every change stays in the public history.

## When a leak or the like occurs

When a leak of personal data or the like gives rise to a situation that the law requires to be reported, the operator reports it to the Personal Information Protection Commission.
The person is told in the issue that person opened.
For something that arrived by email, the person is told at the email address it came from.
A takeover of the operator's GitHub account that changes issues is treated as such a situation.
The reading of the law is under "When a leak or the like occurs" in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md).

## Requests

Anyone may ask for correction, removal, disclosure or a stop to the use of an entry about themselves.
There is no fee.
The procedure is in [Requests](./requests.en.md).
Requests are also received at the email address of the Disclaimer, besides the form.
Complaints about the handling of personal data are received through the same form or at the same email address.

## Age

No proposal is accepted from a person under 16 years of age.
A proposal from a person under 18 years of age is accepted only with the consent of a parent or guardian.
When a proposal turns out to come from a person under 16 years of age, the operator removes the issue without waiting for a request.
