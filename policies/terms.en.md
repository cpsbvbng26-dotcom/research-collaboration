# Terms of collaboration

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

This page sets out the terms between the operator and the people who carry out research with the operator.
The terms apply between the operator and anyone who ticks the boxes of the proposal form.

## Fees and consideration

The operator charges no fee to anyone who collaborates.
The operator pays no wages, no honorarium and no other consideration to anyone who collaborates.
Each side bears the cost of its own work.
The operator does not send the results of a collaboration to a venue that charges publication or submission fees.
Nor does the operator answer solicitations that claim to be free.

## Not employment

The collaboration is not employment.
The operator does not set the hours or the place of any work.
The operator concludes no labour contract with anyone.
The reading of the law, and where no conclusion is drawn, are under "Labour Standards Act and Labour Contracts Act" in [Reading of the law](./law.en.md).

## Voluntary

The collaboration is voluntary for both sides.
Either side may stop at any time without giving a reason.
Stopping costs neither side anything.
What has already been published stays under its licence after a stop.
An unpublished draft written together is not published without the other party's agreement.
How it is handled is decided in the issue of that collaboration.

## How it proceeds

Proposals are received through the [collaboration proposal form](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/research-collaboration/issues/new?template=collaboration.yml).
The operator reads every proposal.
The operator decides whether to take a proposal up, and writes the reason in the issue.
The arrangement of an accepted collaboration (scope, roles, venue, how names are shown) is written in that issue.
A change to the arrangement is written in the same issue.

## What is not taken up

- Research involving human participants. The operator has no route to an ethics review.
- Research that uses other people's special care-required personal information, such as records of patients or service users.
- Publishing claims about diagnosis or treatment.
- Sending work to a venue that charges publication or submission fees.
- Proposals from a person under 16 years of age.

A proposal from a person under 18 years of age is accepted only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## Conduct

The [code of conduct](./conduct.en.md) applies to the collaboration.

## Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Japan.
Disputes fall to the courts that have jurisdiction under the law.
Disputes are first settled by discussion.

## Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits a liability that the law does not allow to be excluded or limited.
The scope of the disclaimer is in [Disclaimer](./disclaimer.en.md).

## Changes

A change to these terms updates the date on this page.
A change that is less favourable to collaborators is shown on this page, and in the issues of ongoing collaborations, before it takes effect.
Earlier versions stay in the public history.
