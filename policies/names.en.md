# Names and rights

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

## The name of this repository

The name of this repository, research-collaboration, is a pair of English words that describe its content.
It is not the name of a person or of an organization.
The operator has not applied to register it as a trademark.
The operator has not searched for trademarks in this name.
The name is used to describe the content, not as a mark of the origin of goods or services.
This repository has no logo and no emblem.

## To rights holders

A rights holder who considers that the name or the text of this repository infringes a trademark, a copyright, a portrait right or another right can notify the operator.
Notices are received through the [rights holder's notice form](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/research-collaboration/issues/new?template=rights.yml).
A notice that cannot be given in public follows "Requests that cannot be made in public" in [Requests](./requests.en.md).
The operator reads every notice.
When the right is confirmed, the operator changes the name or stops using the text.
The decision is recorded in the issue and in the public history.
There is no fee.

## Other people's names

A contributor's name is shown only in the form its holder chooses.
No one is named as a collaborator without that person's agreement.
